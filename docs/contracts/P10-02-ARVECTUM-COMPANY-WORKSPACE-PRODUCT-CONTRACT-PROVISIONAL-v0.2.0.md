## 2. Human-readable effective scope

Within the current `Local / Persistent Internal / owner-operated` scope, this Provisional contract admits bounded implementation and real validation of the following product/platform boundary.

### 2.1 Company asset admission

`company.asset.admit-staged-version` may govern canonical admission of one exact reviewed `StagedNonCanonical` Company-held material version after current Authorization, Organizational Authority, Data Governance, validation and applicable Consequential Approval gates succeed.

The first-slice Company asset classes are limited to:

- brandbook;
- logo and bounded Company brand assets;
- Company document/presentation templates;
- bounded Company-held source/reference materials;
- governed external references;
- an exact project-bound generated document only through the separate reviewed-promotion path when that operation becomes available.

Staging or persistence alone does not create canonical asset state.

### 2.2 External reference admission

`company.asset.admit-external-reference` may govern admission of a Company-relevant reference while preserving the external source as authoritative within the declared `External Reference` scope.

A local cache, copy, extraction, summary or generated representation does not silently become `Native` authority for the underlying external fact/content scope.

### 2.3 Generated-output promotion boundary

`company.generated-output.promote-reviewed` is an admitted contract boundary for later governed promotion of one exact reviewed `TransientOutput` into a governed Company Document/Artifact version and, where declared, a Governed Organizational Asset designation.

**Real reliance on this operation remains unavailable until P10.05 implements and reviews the promotion path.**

Opening, downloading, editing, commenting on or repeatedly using a generated draft does not promote it. Generated output remains `TransientOutput` by default.

### 2.4 Actionable Work projection

`workspace.actionable-work.project-request` is read-only and non-canonical. It may project an explicit current Company/product-owned request into Workspace, but it may not manufacture a request, urgency, assignment, approval, permission or Organizational Authority.

### 2.5 Product-operation entry

`workspace.product-operation.enter` is a routing/entry envelope with no product side effect by itself.

Any actual Tender Agent, Discount Parser or other product consequential effect requires the owning product's exact effective Product Contract plus its own current governed workflow/operation and gate evaluation. If no such effective product boundary exists, Workspace must stop with a truthful blocked/unavailable state.

## 3. Binding authority and safety invariants

This Provisional contract preserves the following boundaries:

1. Authentication, Authorization, Organizational Authority, Data Governance, validation and Consequential Approval remain distinct controls;
2. technical access or UI presence does not create organizational authority or legal right;
3. current gates are re-evaluated at the consequential operation boundary;
4. residual Organizational Authority remains with the owner unless a separately approved exact delegation applies;
5. exact mutable governed inputs and materially relied-upon Product Contract/workflow/control versions are pinned for consequential reliance;
6. external authority remains external where declared;
7. generated artifacts remain `TransientOutput` until a separate governed promotion succeeds;
8. admitted documents/extractions/summaries do not automatically become validated Knowledge;
9. canonical Events/provenance are preserved where required by the consequential operation; ordinary UI/telemetry remains non-canonical by default;
10. replay/reconstruction does not repeat a consequential external effect without new applicable authorization and Governed Execution.

## 4. Binding exclusions and non-claims

This Provisional contract does not authorize or establish:

- executable/script/installer or uncontrolled active-content admission in the first slice;
- arbitrary local-disk/home-directory indexing;
- automatic external sending, signing, filing or publication;
- electronic-signature or legal-validity decisions;
- automatic conversion to Policy, Standard, Decision or validated Knowledge;
- cross-Organization admission, sharing or reuse;
- silent canonicalization through persistence, preview, download, repeated use, AI confidence or successful generation;
- arbitrary Tender Agent, Discount Parser or other product side effects;
- roadmap authority for Workspace projections/caches;
- browser/BFF authority or public/stable `/bff/*` compatibility;
- remote host execution authority;
- Product Contract `Stable` status;
- Platform Capability `Active` status;
- customer/external Production readiness;
- SLA, support, certification or broader conformance commitments;
- legal ownership or expanded reuse rights merely through asset designation.
