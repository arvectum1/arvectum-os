## 2. Approval basis

Phase 9 / M9 is canonically `Complete / Achieved — PASS` for the exact `Local / Persistent Internal / owner-operated` Productive Workspace scope.

The owner explicitly directed on `2026-08-27` that a detailed Phase 10 roadmap be formed and implementation proceed.

The activation review found the selected Phase 10 direction to be the minimum sufficient evidence-backed successor because real M9/F11 use already demonstrates:

- useful but non-canonical staged Company material intake;
- useful exact-version generated documents that remain `TransientOutput` by default;
- a real need for explicit organizational-asset admission/reuse;
- a remaining natural real-work need to exercise a genuine task/action through the governed action path;
- no need for a new foundational RFC or Constitution amendment to begin this bounded work.

## 3. Exact approved scope

Approved scope is limited to the existing internal owner-operated contour and includes:

- defining the authority/admission matrix for initial Company asset classes;
- evolving the required Product Contract boundary before new governed reliance;
- implementing a domain-neutral canonical organizational-asset admission path consistent with RFC-0002/RFC-0005/RFC-0008;
- implementing owner-facing Company Asset Library/version/review UX;
- implementing a reviewed generated-output promotion path while keeping generation transient by default;
- representing genuine product/company Action Requests truthfully without introducing a universal Kernel Task primitive;
- completing at least one naturally occurring genuine governed operational action when such a real request exists;
- source-grounded use of admitted assets without automatic validated-Knowledge promotion;
- real owner dogfooding and pre-closure engineering/code-health gates.

## 4. Explicitly not approved by this decision

This decision does not approve:

- Constitution or Accepted RFC amendment;
- a new Kernel primitive;
- customer/public Production;
- universal multi-tenancy or second-Organization operation;
- a public/stable API, SDK or browser compatibility contract;
- Stable Product Contract transition;
- Active Platform Capability transition;
- automatic admission of staged files or generated outputs;
- automatic document-to-Knowledge promotion;
- AI final approval or Organizational Authority;
- electronic signature, legal filing or external document publication/delivery;
- arbitrary product remote execution;
- 1С/CRM/СЭД business writes;
- SLA/support/certification/conformance expansion.

## 5. Governance conditions

1. New governed product/platform reliance requires the applicable effective Product Contract before use.
2. Consequential canonical asset mutation uses RFC-0005 Governed Execution.
3. Receipt/generation remains distinct from canonical admission.
4. Product-specific semantics remain product-owned.
5. AI does not become authority or approval source.
6. INT-B7 remains blocked until a real external endpoint/deployment/account exists.
7. M10 closure requires the R36 Milestone Code Health Gate before P10.12 closure.
8. Any lifecycle promotion requires its own separately sufficient evidence and decision.

## 6. Decision result

> **Approved — Phase 10 is Active.**
